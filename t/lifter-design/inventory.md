## Appendix: method and caveats

- Records: 13 chunk files, 126 records, no duplicate file names, every file present in census.json, census_in_fragment equal to the census's in_fragment on all 126.
- Loops, invariants, functions and gradable-method counts come from structured fields (loops[].guard, loops[].invariants, loops[].decreases_stated, functions[].kind/recursive/decreases_stated). A decreases_stated of "" or "(none stated ...)" counts as none. Guard shape is the comparison operator of the guard after stripping parentheses; a for-loop is its own shape.
- nat positions, return forms, constructs categories and hazard themes come from keyword rules over free text (agg/common.py, agg/constructs.py, agg/hazards.py). The return form compares the returned expression with the method's declared return names, so `return r;` is a no-op only when r is that method's return.
- Assert sites are counted from the readers' line lists or stated numbers; where the reader's verdict states a larger number (mockExam2_p6 lists 9 lines and says ten) the stated number is used. Asserts in Main are excluded.
- The hazard ranking counts programs whose recorded text raises a theme; it is a measure of how often the readers named the hazard, not of how often the construct occurs in the corpus.
- Nothing here is a kernel measurement beyond what the readers measured on dafny 4.11.0; the other six kernels were absent on this box during the reading, and verus did not run (exit 128).
