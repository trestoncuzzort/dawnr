# Lifting released verified corpora into t, 2026-09-26

locallm's fine-tune memorizes its ~300-document corpus (train 0.11 against held-out 0.68
nats per token at step 300). Regularizers cannot fix a 50K-token corpus; more verified,
English-headed documents can. This is what two released corpora of verified Dafny programs
with their own English yield through t's lifter (`t/lift_corpora.py`, `t/lifter.py`).

## What is not measured

- The seven-kernel verdict (clean in all seven) for every accepted task: unmeasured until
  `bash t/r12_data_queue.sh lift-2026-09-26` runs on the grading machine. It first reruns the
  lifter's own check stage (equivalence lemmas and the differential run under dafny), drops
  every task that fails it, then grades the rest as the 785 DafnyBench lifts were graded.
- dafny-disco (metareflection, 81,342 synthetic Dafny programs, CC-BY-SA-4.0): not lifted; the
  share-alike licence cannot be carried by this repository, the programs are class- and
  datatype-heavy (outside t's fragment), and the parquet needs pyarrow, which no machine here has.
- ATLAS (arXiv:2512.10173): no released data.
- Clover and DafnyBench: already lifted (t/COVERAGE-lifted-785.md, t/HEADS-2026-09-25.md).

## Graded (2026-09-26, lab, 12 cells)

The lifter's check stage kept 233 of the 373 lifted tasks (207 agree with their source on the
differential run; 26 have no differential arm and rest on the equivalence lemmas) and refused 140
(104 lemma not proved or check file broken, 32 well-formedness, 2 differential disagreements, 2
errors). run_par graded the 233 in all seven kernels: **108 clean in all seven**, 37 more in six
(rocq alone keeps out 14, lean 11, framac 7, spark 3, verus 2). Table: `t/COVERAGE-lifted-2026-09-26.md`.

Into the corpus (`loop_locallm.py corpus --lifted --lifted-set
t/out/lifted-tasks-2026-09-26=t/COVERAGE-lifted-2026-09-26.md --heads <the lift's heads, clean rows
only> --split t/out/loop/split-v5.json`): every one of the 108 passes the held-out, dev,
decontamination and behavioural gates. The lifted-and-committed corpus goes from 194 documents
(66,569 characters) to **302 documents (128,793 characters)**; 44 of the new documents carry an
English head (HumanEval-Dafny 9, vericoding 35).

## Regraded with the lowering fixes (2026-09-27)

After the Lean (matching-loop instantiation), Rocq (targeted tactics) and Frama-C (spec-function
mirrors, exact concatenation) fixes, every lifted set was regraded on the lab
(`t/COVERAGE-lifted-*-regrade-2026-09-27.md`; unchanged kernel cells came from the cache). Rows clean
in all seven kernels across the nine sets: 164 before, **237 after**, none lost; clean in six or
more: 246 before, 291 after. The corpus built from the regraded tables: **431 documents clean in
seven** (241,628 characters; 118 with an English head), and 515 with `--min-kernels 6` (84 with a
recorded gap).
