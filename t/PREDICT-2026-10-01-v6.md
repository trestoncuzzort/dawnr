## A measurement on the way, 2026-10-01 18:26Z: why the Python-first route keeps few specifications

The route now records every specification it scores (`spec_first.py`, `scored-specs.jsonl`). The 4B on v6 on
dev: tested Python for 55 of 100 problems (v5: 51); of 189 specifications it wrote from that Python, 144
contradict the problem's own examples (SAFE's correctness below 0.8, 46 problems), 10 are too weak, 14 are
duplicates, 4 cannot be scored, and 17 are kept (10 problems; v5: 15). Read by hand, the contradicting ones are
wrong logic, not notation: `r == len(s) * (len(s) + 1) / 2` for "count the substrings that start and end with
the same character", indices used without being bound, a clause that demands every long suffix be in the
answer. With a right program in view, the student still does not say what the program does. The two steps
queued next aim at exactly this: the specification repair with the contradicted example as the witness
(`t/PREDICT-2026-10-01-spec-repair.md`, 51 and 52) and the specification round's rows written from reference
Python (`t/PREDICT-2026-10-01-spec-round.md`). Predictions 79 and 80 are scored when the answers are graded.
