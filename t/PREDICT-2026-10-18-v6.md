## Candidate and data

compute, rank 64, alpha 16, dropout 0.1, all linear layers, learning rate 2e-4,
five epochs, batch 1 with 16 accumulation steps, maximum row length 2,845,
and seed 1. The trainer is `student_sft.py` at commit `a961cfba`. The input is

`e14f766f06299d589d9a6e3293f6bf3e3f06ffac026433e2fdeff1c04294af80`.

This is not v5's original 5,095-row input with rows appended. It combines the
corrected 3,352-row reconstruction with later data and deduplicates the result.

test results are not formal proofs. The remaining rows include specification
and repair examples and do not all represent independent proved programs.
The exact construction, provenance, deduplication, and panel checks must be
reproduced before release. A zero overlap count from the t-program audit alone
does not establish that Python rows or unparsed examples are independent.

## Comparison and release conditions

The recorded v5 reference is 17/182 at one or more kernels, 12/182 at all seven,
and 27/33 supplied specifications at all seven. These are historical results
to reproduce from saved evidence or remeasure under a common instrument, not
numbers to assume. The held-out route has 17 attempts per problem; the precise
prompts, routes, decoding settings, and model formats must be recovered and
frozen before either model's new held-out generation. Development evaluation
comes first. No held-out failure may guide the candidate's training or selection.

Predictions and acceptance conditions:

- V1: v6 solves at least one more clean-182 problem than v5 under the same
  evaluation, and at least 18 in total. Its all-seven count is at least v5's
  matched count and at least 12.
- V2: v6 proves at least as many of the 33 supplied specifications as v5 under
  the matched evaluation, and at least 27, with all seven kernels.
- V3: the installed-format candidate retains the qualifying results; an
  adapter-only result is insufficient to promote a quantized release.

Both arms use identical problem panels, prompt construction, decoding settings,
attempt budgets, and verifier/specification gates. Report missing outputs,
timeouts, parser failures, refutations, and contamination separately. Record
per-problem outcomes and uncertainty; a one-problem gain on one training seed
does not establish a general improvement across seeds.

If the candidate fails, retain v5 as the installed release and report the
failure. Further development must use development evidence and an evaluation
plan that does not select against exposed held-out outcomes. Do not relabel a
failed candidate as an achieved improvement.
