# English heads for the lifted documents, 2026-09-25

Built by `t/heads_from_sources.py` from sources that exist; nothing written by a model.
Each source has its own curation, stated in every row's `curation` field. A task whose
coverage row is not clean in all seven kernels is not a corpus document and gets no row.

## What was found (r12 data build, plan B.2, track W4)

The run below was made on the lab on 2026-09-25, CPU only, over the 326 lifted tasks,
the coverage table's 192 clean rows, pool v5 and `split-v5.json`; its rows are
`t/out/loop/heads-2026-09-25.jsonl` (49 rows, sha256
`1aec5c99b6467b4a8867efba55ea4cfca76d908d51bd0dee30860cdd6e4ba7b1`, untracked under
`t/out`). `loop_locallm.py corpus --lifted --heads` on the same inputs built 194 documents
(167 lifted, 27 committed) with exactly 49 `Problem:` lines, and 0 without `--heads`.

- **MBPP-DFY (29 of 57 documents).** The test is the one the runner applies to a model's
  answer: the lifted program against MBPP problem N's own points in the t interpreter.
  16 documents have no head because MBPP N is not in the pool (its assertions never parsed
  into t values), 6 are refused by the gates (269 held out; 242, 404, 728 and the rest are
  same-task exclusions), 5 fail or mismatch the points (arity 1, type 1, fail 3), and 1
  passes only inside its `requires` and is not counted as passing.
- **Clover (18 of 23 documents).** All 28 lifted Clover stems have a docstring in the
  dataset; 5 clean documents are same-task exclusions and get none. The docstrings are
  the dataset authors' one-line descriptions, kept as written, including one that names a
  second return value the lifter dropped (`multi_return`) and one that says "Use twostate
  specifiation" (`replace`).
- **DafnyBench-derived (2 of 112 documents).** This is the finding. 73 of the 112 clean
  documents have no comment on the method at all; 12 have a comment that a blank line
  separates from the header (exercise numbers, student names, `RUN:` lines, one
  two-word description, "Incrementing arrays"); of the 27 with a comment directly on the
  method, 8 are one or two words ("Ex 1.3", "Problem 01", "Ou encore:"), 5 are formulas,
  Hoare triples or commented-out clauses, 5 are not English (Portuguese, Spanish, a
  diagram), 8 are instructions to the student or notes about the proof ("Annotate this
  method with pre- and postconditions", "do not change", "Exercise 1. Write a test
  method"), and 1 English description ("Return a minimum of a.") is a same-task
  exclusion. What remains is two fragments of the same exercise file ("Algorithm 1: From
  left to right return the first"). The 112 DafnyBench-derived documents therefore stay
  head-less: their source carries no English for them, and no head is invented. The
  meta-commentary list that refused the 8 instructions was written after reading every
  candidate of this run; each is named below with its outcome.

So the corpus's head-less documents drop from 222 to 222 - 49 = 173 with this file, all of
the gain from the two sources whose English was written for the program (MBPP, Clover).
The remaining English for the DafnyBench programs would have to come from a teacher and
be curated by test, which is what section B.2 of the plan describes and this track does
not do.
