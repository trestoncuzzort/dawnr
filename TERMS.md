## Claims, reviews, corrections

rated, endorsed or reviewed by anyone, and no review is shown. Claims that were withdrawn, and why, are in
[CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).

## Your machine

The assistant changes files only as a plan you were shown, journals every change so that it can be undone, runs
commands in a sandbox, and never grants itself permissions ([DAWNR-AGENT.md](DAWNR-AGENT.md)). You are
responsible for what you ask it to do and for the files you give it. dawnr sends them nowhere ([PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

## Nothing hidden

The page has one button and it does what it says. Nothing is pre-selected that costs you anything, nothing nags,
nothing is bought, no consent is asked for because no data is taken. dawnr sends no email.

## Accessibility

The page uses the system's own fonts and ships no images, so there is nothing to license there. Its text stands
at a contrast of 4.5:1 or more against its background in the light and the dark scheme, and its controls at 3:1
(WCAG 2.2 level AA, checked on 2026-10-06). Every control can be reached and used from the keyboard, and results
are announced to screen readers. If something is not, report it the same way as a wrong number.
