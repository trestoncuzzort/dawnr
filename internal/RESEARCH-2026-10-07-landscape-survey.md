- **Public benchmarks saturate within a year.** CLEVER went from 1/161 to 98.1% (entries with self-consistent premises), VERINA proof from 4.9% (o3) to 96.8% resolved (Aristotle, counting 23 disproved), DafnyBench from 68% to 92.7%. AlgoVeri is the unsaturated one.
- **Aligned multi-verifier benchmarks stop at three.** VerifyThisBench has seven tools but tool-specific tasks; only Dafny, Verus and Frama-C overlap with t's seven.
- **Spec quality is now measured.** LeetProof found 16 of 189 VERINA specs (8.5%) and 18 of 161 CLEVER specs (11.2%) defective by property-based testing (https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16584).

## 3. LLMs with formal verification, reported results

- **Dafny.** AxDafny 92.7% on DafnyBench (30 Jun 2026); its paper names https://github.com/Axiomatic-AI/ax-dafny, which returned 404 on 2026-10-07 (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.32007). Reinforcement learning on Dafny lifted verified reward "from 2.2% to 58.1%" with specification hacking (29 May 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30914). Neuro-formal verification (v3 14 Sep 2026): Dafny resolves 57% of entries at 92% precision, while an unstaged agent plus verifier "proves 98% of both the correct and the known-buggy programs" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21516).
- **Verus.** AutoVerus "more than 90%" of 150 tasks (v3 22 Aug 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13082); SAFE 52.52% vs GPT-4o 14.39% (v3 14 Feb 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15756); AlphaVerus (9 Dec 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06176); VeriStruct 128 of 129 functions (v4 3 Mar 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25015); IDS 7/7 distributed key-value specs in "about 6.8 hours and $106" against 2/7 for Codex and Claude Code (22 May 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23109).
- **Lean, code.** Goedel-Code-Prover 8B: Verina 68.8%, Clever 54.0%, AlgoVeri 62.3%, "62.0% across all 427 tasks" (v3 10 Aug 2026; code and spec given for Verina, code for the other two written by GPT-5.2; https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19329). P3: +4.6 to 11.2 points on Verina, AlgoVeri and Lean4Commit0 (10 Aug 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09277). Leanstral, an Apache-2.0 Lean agent (16 Mar 2026, https://mistral.ai/news/leanstral). A "99% (187 of 189)" Verina result for Axiom appears only in a secondary summary (https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/formal-verification-and-verified-ai-for-mathematical-reasoning-at-scale); primary not found.
- **Lean, mathematics (no code).** DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B "88.9%" miniF2F-test, "49 out of 658" PutnamBench (v2 18 Jul 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21801); Kimina-Prover Preview "80.7%" miniF2F at pass@8192 (15 Apr 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11354); Goedel-Prover-V2-32B 90.4% miniF2F with self-correction (5 Aug 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03613); Seed-Prover 1.5 "88%" PutnamBench, 11 of 12 Putnam 2025 problems (19 Dec 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17260); AxiomProver 12 of 12 on Putnam 2025 (post 8 Jan 2026, https://axiommath.ai/research/from-seeing-why-to-checking-everything); Aristotle, IMO 2025 gold-medal equivalent (10 Oct 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01346).
- **SPARK.** Marmaragan 50.7% with GPT-4o (11 Feb 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07728). "The Prover Is the Judge" (15 Jul 2026): agents wrote 77.7 kLOC and GNATprove discharged 49,280 obligations; the agent used pragma Assume "to silence a proof obligation", then SPARK_Mode => Off, and a transposed SSH key derivation "passed every obligation" (https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14340). AdaCore, 14 Apr 2026: the agent "crashed and burned" on Silver proofs and "littered my code with `pragma Annotate`s"; the proof effort surfaced bugs in the original C++ (a near-infinite or negative sensor footprint) although its tests reached 98% coverage (https://www.adacore.com/blog/challenging-agentic-ai-with-spark-using-openuxas).
- **Frama-C, C.** CASP: 506 C and ACSL pairs (26 Aug 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18798); in one-shot ACSL generation "rule-based approaches remain more reliable" than DeepSeek-V3.2, GPT-5.2 or OLMo 3.1 (v3 14 Apr 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13851); SpecAgent 96.82% precision, 88.95% recall, 604 of 811 targets (4 Oct 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05132).
- **Rocq, F\*.** AutoRocq on SV-COMP and Linux kernel modules (v3 11 Apr 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17330). F*: only the 2024 Microsoft dataset, where fine-tuned small models "compare favorably" with GPT-4 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01787); no 2025-26 F* code-generation number found.

## 4. Where t is unusual, and where competitors lead

**Unusual (nothing found in this search does the same).**
1. One source lowered mechanically into seven independent verifiers, SMT-based and kernel-based, graded cell by cell. The widest aligned benchmarks have three (section 1). hax and Aeneas reach four to six targets but start from Rust, and the pages read show no per-task agreement table. Federated Formal Verification gates TLA+ obligations across kernels, with no lowered program.
2. Refusal by name with sole-blocker accounting; hax's stable and experimental badges are the nearest.
3. The twin as a kernel-accepted refutation certificate at a measured witness, in every leg. The twin itself is no longer unique. SWE-Proof (v2 22 Sep 2026) ships a "pre-fix twin" that "must fail to verify": "A specification whose twin verifies is not admitted" (600 twins for Nagini, 569 for Velvet, 540 for Lean), plus a mutation check requiring "a high kill rate" (https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21190). SpecSyn scores spec strength by mutants refuted (23 Apr 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21570); MutDafny reads a verifying mutant as a weak spec (https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15403). What stays different: a one-edit mechanical twin, a certificate instead of a bare verifier failure, the same twin across all legs.

**The 10-06 landscape note needs four corrections.** "None pairs each proof with a refuted mutant": SWE-Proof does, on three backends. "No system targets more than three verifiers": true only for aligned tasks; VerifyThisBench has seven. "Lean is the weak leg on every multi-verifier benchmark": true for one-shot writing, but agents reach 95.0% and 98.1% (section 1). AxDafny "paper only": the paper names a repository, 404 today.

**Independence is weaker than the table suggests.** Dafny sits on Boogie (Z3 default) and Verus on Z3 (https://verus-lang.github.io/verus/guide/), which also says it does not intend to "verify the verifier itself". SPARK runs on Why3 with CVC5 and Z3 (https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14340); Frama-C WP lists Alt-Ergo, Coq, Z3, CVC4 (https://frama-c.com/fc-plugins/wp.html). Lean's reference documents independent re-checkers, comparator with nanoda and lean4checker (https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/ValidatingProofs/); the pages read show no analogue for the SMT-backed legs.

**Clearly ahead of t.**
- Scale and real code: SWE-Proof's 500 real issues, VeruSAGE's 849 tasks, upstream-accepted KVerus proofs, 77.7 kLOC of SPARK, Vericoding's 12,504 specs, against t's 88 tasks.
- Language reach: Aeneas handles safe Rust with loops and Viper reasons with permissions. t has no heap, and heaps, classes or graphs are why 23 of AlgoVeri's 77 contracts are unstated (`t/algoveri/README.md`).
- Agentic proof search is saturating Lean and Dafny benchmarks; t has no prover in the loop.
- Foundational trust: a verified Dafny VCG and compiler in HOL4, Lean-on-Dafny's verified weakest-precondition generator, Loom and Velvet.
- Shipping code: Dafny's five targets and SPARK deliverables.

## 5. Expansion candidates, ranked by impact over effort (I = impact, E = effort)

1. **Lean loops through `mvcgen` and `grind`, probe first. I medium-high, E low for the probe.** Lean's tutorial says `mvcgen` "does require an invariant for the loop", after which `all_goals mleave; grind` closes the rest (https://lean-lang.org/doc/tutorials/4.32.2/mvcgen/); t's loops already carry invariants. `t/lower_lean.py` mentions `grind` 341 times and `mvcgen` never. On AlgoVeri, Lean carries 10 of 21, verifies 0, and co-blocks 20 (`t/ALGOVERI.md`); LeetProof's Velvet pipeline (28/50 against 17/50 for single-mode Lean) suggests what Dafny-style automation inside Lean buys. Bar: integer_exponential (the one task Lean alone keeps out of all seven) and bubble_sort verify in Lean with the twin refuted.
2. **Common-mode audit. I medium, E low.** Re-run the SMT legs under a second solver (Boogie's experimental CVC5, Why3's cvc5 and Alt-Ergo) and replay Lean cells through comparator with nanoda; report agreement under solver change. Evidence: the shared Z3 above and the Lean reference. `t/RELATED-WORK.md` section 3 already proposes the Lean half.
3. **A model writing t, against AlgoVeri's per-language baselines. I high, E medium.** AlgoVeri v2 holds the only aligned baselines (Gemini-3 Flash 40.26%, 24.68%, 7.79% after 15 rounds). Give a model the same 21 contracts and 15 rounds with t as the language; the seven kernels grade it and the twin replaces the LLM judge that cost Dafny about 15 points. With n = 21 the intervals are wide. Bar: the Dafny leg matches direct Dafny, and the Verus and Lean legs beat direct Verus and Lean. This tests the central claim that writing one language buys seven. It needs a model budget and the operator's go-ahead, since assistant work is paused.
4. **Lift DafnyComp's 300 chained programs. I medium-high, E medium.** Models fall from ">58%" to "3.69%" when functions compose (https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23061v1). t has methods, lemmas and higher-order calls, and per-function twins give a compositional agreement table. Start with 30.
5. **Twin-audit public benchmark specs. I high, E medium-high.** LeetProof's 8.5% and 11.2% (property-based tests), Vericoding's "roughly 9%" (manual reading), SWE-Proof's "only 56%" of agent-written specs passing audit (LLM auditors, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21190) and Aristotle's 23 false VERINA statements show defective specs are common; none grades them by kernel-checked twin refutation across kernels. Lift what t can state (Vericoding's Dafny files include VERINA, CLEVER and DafnyBench sources) and publish kernel-checked weak-spec counts.
6. **Records and options (G9) first, measured on AlgoVeri. I high, E high; already queued.** 37 of the 55 unstated contracts need datatypes with fields, 23 heaps, classes or graphs, 7 a quantifier over all sequences (`t/algoveri/README.md`); Goedel-Code-Prover and P3 already score all 77 in Lean.
7. **One foundational column. I medium, E high.** Lower to Velvet or Strata Core so one leg's verification conditions have Lean semantics, answering "agreement is not proof" on the precedent of Lean-on-Dafny and the HOL4 VCG. Velvet is v0.1.0 and Strata warns of "breaking changes" (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/strata-org/Strata/main/README.md), so this follows 1-6.

Held back, outside the current focus: agent-filled invariant slots under the twin gate (AxDafny 92.7%, CLEVER 98.1%). "The Prover Is the Judge" shows agents bypass weak checks, which t's no-assume, no-sorry rules and the twin already target.
