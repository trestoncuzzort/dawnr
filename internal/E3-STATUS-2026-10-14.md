## Pipeline, verified end-to-end this session (module names/flags confirmed on the lab)

1. **Teacher (answers).** `bedrock_generate.py --tag <tag> --model <permissive-model> --samples K` over the
   368-task MBPP train pool (`spec_experiment.pool()`), records `raw/<task_id>.json` under
   `out/spec-experiment/<tag>/`. IMPORTANT licence constraint: the default model is Claude Sonnet 4.5;
   training ROWS must come from an Apache/MIT teacher (DeepSeek-V3.2 via Bedrock), NOT Claude/GPT — the
   held-out/contamination audit admits permissive licences only.
2. **Extract + tests.** `spec_experiment.py extract --model <tag>`; `… tests --model <tag>`.
3. **Grade through 7 kernels.** `run_par.py --tasks out/spec-experiment/<tag>/tasks --out …/kernels
   --table …/kernels.md` (dafny, verus, spark, framac, lean, rocq, fstar on the lab's 120 cores).
4. **Build arms + audit.** `e3_build_arms.py out/spec-experiment/<tag> <out-dir>` → raw / graded-k<K> /
   all7 SFT rows, de-duplicated, with size/diversity printed for budget-matching; then `heldout_audit.py
   --panels --refuse-gpl` (permissive licences only, contamination/held-out gate).
5. **Train.** `student_sft.py` QLoRA (4-bit NF4, rank 64, lr 2e-4, 5 epochs) on a 0.5–1.5B Apache/MIT base
   (e.g. Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B-Instruct, Apache-2.0), 3 arms × 3 seeds = 9 fine-tunes, matched token budget
   and matched diversity — on a `g5.xlarge` in us-east-1.
6. **Eval.** pass@1/pass@k on Vericoding (disjoint from the MBPP train pool) + twin survival on the 426
   pairs.
