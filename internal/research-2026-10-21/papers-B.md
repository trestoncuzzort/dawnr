# Research scout B: papers for dawnr's verification training, proofs, provers and benchmarks

- Date (UTC): 2026-10-21
- Method: 90 arXiv API queries (export.arxiv.org over https, up to 40 results each, a 3-second pause between calls, Atom XML parsed with xml.etree under python3 -I), plus one id_list lookup to confirm exact titles for WebSearch-only ids. The pool held 1,477 unique arXiv records. Records were kept by keyword (verification, proof, specification, autoformalization and benchmark terms), deduplicated by arXiv id and normalized title, and then selected by hand for D4, D5, D6 and D8 relevance. 20 WebSearch queries (standard mode) covered venue pages (PLDI, POPL, CAV, OOPSLA, ICSE, FSE, ASE, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, TACAS, ITP, CPP, FM, VSTTE) and 2026 work. A venue other than arXiv is stated only where a search result showed it.
- Issues: D4 training data for verification models; D5 invariant, annotation and proof synthesis; D6 multiple provers and kernels; D8 benchmarks and evaluation.
- Totals: 250 distinct entries, 246 with arXiv links and 4 with conference or workshop links. 22 entries show a venue other than arXiv. Per-issue counts: D4 = 72, D5 = 149, D6 = 69, D8 = 116 (an entry can carry more than one issue).
